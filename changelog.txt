0.2.0: Info card. For each mod you can tick Favorite, pick a type (Gear, Hair, Body, Tattoo, Jewelry, Emote, FX, Furniture, Pet, Other), and fill in Made for, your own tags and notes. Everything you type is searchable. New filters: type (including "not set") and Favorites only. Favorites get a gold star in the list.
0.1.1: Arrow keys no longer walk your character. Click a mod (or the search box) and the up and down arrows move through the list; Page Up and Page Down jump 10. Also added Previous and Next buttons.
0.1.0: First version. A searchable list of every mod in your Penumbra folder, in your own Penumbra folder order. Search names, folders, authors, tags and descriptions; filter by top folder; scroll with the arrow keys. Shows where each mod sits in Penumbra, with Copy name and Copy full path buttons. /lookingglass or /lglass to open.
